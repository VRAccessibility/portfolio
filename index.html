Craig Anderton

Award-Winning HCI PhD Candidate A11y Researcher

Objective Statement

I am currently researching sensory augmentation to make virtual reality more accessible for people with sensory impairments. My work is published in leading HCI venues, including a first-author full paper at CHI '25, a first-author article in the Q1 IJHCI journal during my first year of PhD research, and a Graduate Gold Medal at the ASSETS '22 Student Research Competition as a MSc student. Drawing on my academic background in mixed-methods research with users with complex access needs and my lived experience of disability, I am dedicated to translating research into practical, accessible solutions for immersive environments.
